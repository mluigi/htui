## Correctness / robustness

- **L1** `crates/htui/src/concepts_worker.rs` ~1867 (`serve`: spawn then abort) and ~1465 (`index_under_lease`
  releases by `press_owner`). Since M3 every press shares `press_owner`, and `serve` spawns the new press before
  aborting the old one: the old press's in-flight `release_indexer_lease(.., press_owner)` can commit after the new
  press's `take` (`Ours`), leaving the new press syncing with no lease. Fix: abort the superseded press task before
  spawning the new one (preferred), or have the new press await the old handle before it takes. Add a test if one
  can pin it deterministically; otherwise say why not.
- **L2** `crates/htui/src/concepts_worker.rs` ~1697-1702 (`stop_index_job`'s press leg): awaiting the aborted tasks
  and the release share one `TUI_RELEASE_WAIT`, so a stuck aborted task skips the release entirely. Fix: bound the
  await by its own share (e.g. `TUI_RELEASE_WAIT / 2`), then always attempt the release within the rest; the leg as a
  whole stays within `TUI_RELEASE_WAIT` (D7's 2 s worst case with the job leg in parallel must not grow).
- **L3** `crates/htui-core/src/model/indexer.rs` ~120-123: `LeaseTurn::Held` doc says "held it live at the last
  attempt", but PgStore's take can answer `Held` with `live: false` after its own `TAKE_ATTEMPTS`. Reword the doc to
  "the holder the last take saw (possibly lapsed)". Doc only.

  promise "the holder … reconciles at its next beat", but a one-off holder (a Ctrl+R press, `--index-items`) never
  claims; the request waits for an index job to take the lease. Reword to: the holder's index job, or the next job to
