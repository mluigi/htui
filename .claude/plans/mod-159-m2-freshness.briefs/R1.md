## What the change read must answer (plan D3/D4, unchanged)

`IndexChangesStore::index_changes(projects, since)` lists, per asked project, rows written strictly after `since`:
`items` (item `updated_at > since`, or an item document version `created_at > since`, listed under the item's
current project), `requirements` (`updated_at > since`), `owner_documents` (documents with no item,
`created_at > since`). Only asked projects appear; an empty `projects` slice answers empty. Postgres (T2,
`crates/htui-store/src/pg/index_changes.rs`) filters every one of its four `UNION` arms by `project_id = ANY($1)`;
MemStore (`crates/htui-core/src/store/mem.rs`, `impl IndexChangesStore for MemStore`) filters each loop by `asked`.
