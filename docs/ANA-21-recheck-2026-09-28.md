> proxy had blocked most benchmark and vendor pages, so §3.3 and §5.7's basis column were built
> from search-result summaries. This file records the second pass: every figure re-read from its

## 9. What the pass changed, and what it did not

No model crosses a tier. The five-tier scheme in §5.1 absorbed every correction, which is the
result the draft predicted and the reason the verdict used coarse tiers.

What the pass changed is the *stated basis* of five seeded rows, none of which any public source
can currently settle: `sonnet` (§7.4), the `gemini-3.8-flash-high` coding tier (§7.5), the
3.7-vs-3.8 version order (§7.6), the seven unmeasured sub-`high` Gemini strings (§7.7), and the
`gemini-3.1-pro-low` effort mismatch (§7.8). Those are maintainer decisions, and they are the
reason ANA-21 stays open.
